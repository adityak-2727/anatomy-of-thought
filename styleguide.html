Proof III A sample plate

Plate I The specimen

Here is our specimen, a small riddle. You probably solved it without noticing. The plates that follow show how a machine arrives at the same answer.

It could mean the trophy or the suitcase. One word near the end decides which.

Proof IV Figure materials

Slips, tape, thread

Each piece is a paper slip, pinned and lettered as on an anatomical plate. Its number feeds out beneath it on ticker tape.

Threads are laid cotton. Their width, and a second ply for the strongest, carry the weight; no line is wider than two pixels.

Only the sensitised slips can be changed by the reader.

Hold the loupe over a figure to see what the machine sees.
